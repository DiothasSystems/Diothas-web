Tip Smart tells you what to tip, in the country you are standing in. If something
is not working the way you expect, the answers below cover the questions we are
asked most. If yours is not here, email us — a real person reads it.

## Contact us

**[tipsmart@diothassystems.com](mailto:tipsmart@diothassystems.com)**

To get a useful answer on the first reply, please include:

- Your **app version** — open **Settings** (the gear icon) and read the line at the very bottom, e.g. `Tip Smart · v1.2.0 (7)`
- Your **device and OS version**, e.g. "Pixel 8 Pro, Android 16"
- The **country and service** you had selected when the problem happened

## Using the app

### Tip Smart has the wrong country

Tap the location box at the top left and choose the right country from the
list. Your choice sticks until you change it or ask for a fresh GPS reading.

If you would rather it detect your location again, open the same box and tap
**Use my location instead**.

### Why does it say a city I am not in?

When Tip Smart cannot get a GPS fix, it falls back to a representative city for
your selected country and labels the reading **manual** rather than **GPS**. It
is telling you it is guessing from the country, not from your position. Tapping
**Use my location instead** will try again.

### Does Tip Smart track or store my location?

No. Tip Smart has no account and keeps no history of where you have been.

When you allow location access, your coordinates are sent once to a third-party
reverse-geocoding service (BigDataCloud) purely to turn them into a country
name. We do not receive, store, or retain your location, and it is not used for
anything else. You can decline location access entirely and pick your country by
hand — every feature still works.

The full detail is in the [Terms & Privacy](/tip-smart/terms/) page.

### Why does the flashlight need camera permission?

On both Android and iOS the torch is part of the camera hardware, so the system
requires camera permission to switch it on. Tip Smart never opens the camera, and
takes no photos or video. If you would rather not grant it, decline — the rest
of the app is unaffected.

### Can I see the feature tour again?

Yes. **Settings → Show feature tour** walks through the main features again from
the beginning.

### How do I change my home currency?

**Settings → Home currency.** This is the currency used for the approximate
equivalent shown next to local amounts. It does not change the tip.

### How do I split a bill?

Tap **Split** below the total, then set the number of people. Tap **Share** to
send the breakdown to a group chat — it shows the bill, the tip, the total, and
what each person owes.

## About the numbers

### Where do the tipping percentages come from?

From a reference dataset covering **73 countries** across 14 service categories,
compiled and checked against published sources. Tap **Info** on any screen to
see the local custom behind the number, including whether a service charge is
usually added already.

Tipping is a custom, not a rule. Norms vary by city, by venue, and by
circumstance, and they change over time. Treat the suggestion as informed
guidance and use your own judgement.

### The converted amount does not match my bank

It will not, and it is not meant to. The equivalent shown in your home currency
is an **indicative estimate for orientation only**. It uses a daily reference
rate and takes no account of your card issuer's spread, ATM fees, or the
merchant's own conversion.

It also never affects the tip. The tip is always calculated in the local
currency of the bill.

### Does the app work without a data connection?

Yes, and it is designed to. All tipping guidance is built into the app. With no
connection you pick your country by hand and Tip Smart uses the last exchange
rates it downloaded, telling you in Settings how old they are.

## Ads and cost

### Is Tip Smart free?

Yes, entirely free, and supported by a single banner ad at the bottom of the
screen. There is no paid tier, no subscription, and no in-app purchase, so there
is currently no way to remove the ad.

### The banner is advertising another app

That is us. When no ad is available to show, the slot displays a cross-promo for
another Diothas Systems app rather than sitting empty.

## Something is wrong

### The app crashed, or a number looks wrong

Please email **[tipsmart@diothassystems.com](mailto:tipsmart@diothassystems.com)** with
the details listed at the top of this page. If a tipping figure looks wrong for a
country you know well, say so and tell us what you would expect — corrections
from people on the ground are genuinely useful, and the dataset gets updated.

### Requesting a country or service we do not cover

Email us. Tell us the country and the kinds of service you would want covered.

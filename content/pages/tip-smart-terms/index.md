## 2. Informational Purpose Only

Tip Smart provides general tipping guidance based on a reference dataset of country and service-category norms. Tipping customs vary by city, venue, and individual circumstance, and may change over time. The App's suggestions are estimates only, are not professional, financial, or legal advice, and are not a substitute for your own judgment. You are solely responsible for any amount you choose to tip.

## 3. Currency Conversion

Where the App shows an amount converted into your selected home currency, that figure is an indicative estimate for orientation only — it is not a quoted or guaranteed rate. Rates are retrieved from a third-party provider, may be cached, delayed, or unavailable, and will differ from the rate your bank, card issuer, or a currency exchange actually applies. Conversions never affect the tip or total calculated in local currency, and the App may omit a converted figure entirely where no reliable rate is available.

## 6. No Account; Stateless Design

The App does not require an account and does not store your bill amounts, tip calculations, or split details after you close it. Your acceptance of this Agreement and basic settings (such as home currency or text size) may be stored locally on your device so you don't have to re-enter them each time.

## 7. What the App Collects, and Why

We do not ask for, and have no way to receive, your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you. We operate no user accounts and no server that stores anything about you.

Handled on your device only:

- **What you enter** — bill amounts, the service you picked, party size, and the resulting tips. These are held in memory while you use the App and are discarded when you close it. They are never transmitted anywhere.
- **Your settings** — home currency, theme, text size, whether you have accepted this Agreement, and the last exchange rates downloaded. These are stored locally on your device.

Three outside services receive a request from the App:

- **BigDataCloud** receives your device's coordinates, and only if you grant location permission — see Section 8.
- **Google AdMob** serves the banner advertisement and may collect your device's advertising identifier and information about ad interactions — see Section 9.
- **open.er-api.com** supplies daily reference exchange rates. This request asks for a published rate table and carries no information about you, your location, or your bill.

We share data with these providers only on the basis that they afford it protection at least equivalent to this Agreement. Each remains subject to its own privacy policy, which governs what it does with what it receives. We do not sell your data, and we have no database in which to keep it.

## 8. Location Services

If you grant permission, the App uses your device's location solely to suggest your current country for tipping guidance. To turn coordinates into a country name, the App sends those coordinates to a third-party reverse-geocoding service (BigDataCloud), which is subject to its own privacy practices. Only a country name comes back, and the App needs nothing finer, so on iOS you may grant approximate location rather than precise and everything still works. We do not receive, store, or retain your location, and it is not used for any other purpose. The App does not track your movements and keeps no history of where you have been. You may decline location access and select your country manually at any time, and you may withdraw permission later as described in Section 11 — every feature still works.

## 9. Advertising and Your Advertising Identifier

The App displays banner advertising served by Google AdMob. On iOS, the App asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location and information about ad interactions, as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Section 11 explains how to withdraw tracking permission and how to reset or delete your advertising identifier. When no ad is available, the App may show a promotion for another Diothas Systems app; that promotion is served from our own site and collects nothing about you.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to retain or to delete. There is no account to close and no record to erase.

Everything the App stores is stored on your device. Bill amounts, tips and splits are discarded as soon as you close the App. Settings and your acceptance of this Agreement persist until you remove them.

**To delete everything the App has stored, uninstall it.** Uninstalling removes all of the App's local data from the device, and reinstalling starts from a clean state.

You may contact us at [tipsmart@diothassystems.com](mailto:tipsmart@diothassystems.com) with any question or request about your data, including a request for deletion, and we will respond within 30 days. In most cases the answer will be that we hold nothing to delete, and we will confirm that to you in writing.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's privacy policy, not ours; Section 11 explains the controls Apple and Google provide.

## 11. Your Choices and How to Withdraw Consent

Every permission the App asks for is optional, and you can change your mind at any time without losing the use of the App.

- **Location** — decline it, or revoke it later under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Tip Smart, or Android Settings → Apps → Tip Smart → Permissions. The App then asks you to pick your country from a list and works identically in every other respect.
- **Camera** — the torch is the only feature that uses it, and no image is ever captured. Revoke it in the same place.
- **Tracking (iOS)** — if you allowed tracking at the App Tracking Transparency prompt, withdraw it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads continue, but stop being personalised.
- **Advertising identifier** — on iOS, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, delete or reset the advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **Everything else** — uninstalling the App removes it, as described in Section 10.
